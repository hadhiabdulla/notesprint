    Offline access, accessibility & honest limits

    The app shell is cached by a service worker on supported HTTPS browsers after the first successful load. Browser eviction, private mode, disabled storage, or an uncached first visit can stop offline access. Keep a deck backup. There is no cloud sync.

    Designed with labelled controls, visible focus, generous tap targets, larger text, no timer and no motion. English interface; card text can use other scripts. No translation, OCR, semantic grading or speech generation. Device and screen-reader compatibility needs testing on your own setup.

    Opening this hosted site makes ordinary requests to the hosting provider, which may receive connection metadata. The app has no analytics, remote fonts, model downloads or note-submission endpoint. Optional remembering uses localStorage; offline caching stores the app, not your notes.

    Built for AccessED Hacks, November 2026. Implementation, design, tests and synthetic examples were made with AI assistance, with limits documented in the repository.
